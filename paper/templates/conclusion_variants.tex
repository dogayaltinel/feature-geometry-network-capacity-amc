\section{Conclusion}
This study defines a controlled framework for examining feature geometry and
network capacity in short-window automatic modulation classification. By
holding the corpus, observation length, backbone family, training schedule,
and evaluation rules fixed, the design makes it possible to report feature
effects, capacity effects, and their interaction as separate quantities. The
final conclusion will be completed from the locked primary and confirmatory
records and will remain bounded by the declared corpus, window, and backbone
scope.
